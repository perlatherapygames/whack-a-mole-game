How to play

1. Listen 👂

Tap “Hear It Again” anytime. Read the words, listen to the voice, and check the steps at the top.

2. Whack in order 👆

For 2-step, do Step 1 first, then Step 2. The step bubbles fill in gold when you get them right.

3. Keep going ⭐

Wrong mole? No problem — you get a gentle hint and can try again. Stars are for finishing, not for being perfect.

📱 Add it to your Home Screen

iPhone / iPad: Tap Share in Safari, then “Add to Home Screen.” It will open full-screen like an app.
Android: Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome, then “Add to Home screen” / “Install app.”

Scores don’t save between visits — every game starts fresh, which is perfect for a new session each time.

For grown-ups & therapists

Use 1-step to work on listening for one key detail (color, animal, or object). Use 2-step for sequencing with “first / then” — the on-screen steps make the order visible while the child listens. Turn Voice off to give the direction yourself, or turn on Bigger Text and Calm Mode for an easier visual field. Repetition is built in: “Hear It Again” is unlimited.
